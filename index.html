LoopFrenzy Control Room

Uses YouTube API Services

LoopFrenzy Control Room is a private tool that the owner of the LoopFrenzy YouTube channel runs on their own computer. It is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads Shorts that the channel owner has edited and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel, at scheduled times.
  • Checks the channel afterwards to confirm that each upload is live.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, average percentage viewed, traffic sources) so the owner can see which Shorts perform best.

How it signs in

The channel owner signs in once with their Google Account using Google's OAuth consent screen. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party access.
